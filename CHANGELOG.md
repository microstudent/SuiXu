## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-19

Initial source baseline; this entry does not imply a published binary release.
首个源码基线；本条记录不代表已经发布二进制安装包。

- Gregorian/lunar calendar and 4,800 offline solar terms for 1901–2100; official mainland China holiday schedules for 2025–2026.
- Compact five/six-row month layout, date details, month/year navigation, keyboard shortcuts, and pinning.
- Date-only menu bar entry, optional weather, and light/dark/system appearance.
- Larger month-navigation hit areas, a consolidated overflow menu, and improved dark-background text contrast.
- Dock visibility follows the Settings window: hidden during ordinary menu bar use, visible while Settings is open.
- 1901–2100 年公历、农历与 4,800 条离线节气；2025–2026 年中国大陆官方放假调休。
- 五／六行紧凑月历、日期详情、年月跳转、快捷键与固定浮层。
- 纯日期菜单栏入口、可选天气及深浅色／跟随系统外观。
- 扩大月份导航热区、合并更多菜单、优化深色背景文字对比度。
- Dock 图标仅在设置窗口打开期间显示。
