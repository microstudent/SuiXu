## Hong Kong Observatory · 香港天文台

`Sources/SuixuCore/Resources/solar-terms.json` contains 4,800 solar-term names and dates extracted from the Observatory's Gregorian–Lunar calendar conversion tables for 1901–2100. Traditional Chinese names were normalized to Simplified Chinese and stored as JSON; the original tables, layout, illustrations, and full text are not bundled.

Source: https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/conversion.htm

The source material is published by the Hong Kong Observatory on the HKSAR Government's website. ## Mainland China holiday schedules · 中国大陆放假调休

`Sources/SuixuCore/Resources/holidays.json` records factual dates and names from the General Office of the State Council's published notices. Each year includes its official source URL and document number. The full original documents and government logos are not bundled. Refer to [DATA](docs/DATA.md) for the specific notices and validation process.

放假调休文件只整理正式通知中的日期、假期名称与补班信息，每年附原文链接及文号；不附带通知全文或政府标识，不暗示政府认可。

## Open-Meteo

Weather is retrieved at runtime only when the user enables it; no weather dataset is bundled. Data is provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The app rounds temperatures and translates weather codes into Chinese labels.

The free API service is for non-commercial use and has rate limits, separately from the data license. Commercial deployments must arrange an appropriate service plan or endpoint; MIT licensing of the app does not grant free commercial API access. See [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

天气仅在用户启用后在线获取，不打包天气数据。数据采用 CC BY 4.0，界面保留来源链接，对温度取整并把天气代码转换为中文。免费 API 的非商业使用限制与数据许可证是两回事，商用分发需自行安排合适的服务方案。
